Francisco has it, nothing more you need to do."), and keeps a resolved-history record the Bags Not On Rack widget now surfaces. Driver-app gained a global alert-banner stack (`loadPendingDriverAlerts`/`subscribeToDriverAlerts`/`renderDriverAlertStack`/`acknowledgeDriverAlert`) driven by `driver_alerts`. In-app only, no SMS fallback (David's call — dispatch already calls drivers directly).

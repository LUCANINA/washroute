The switch

What "Program is off" does when you click it

It is the master switch, and it is enforced in the database, not the interface — so a stale browser tab can't work around it.

Off → On
Codes start being minted, the card appears wherever you've ticked a surface, and /r/CODE links begin working. Nothing is sent to anyone — customers have to go find it.
On → Off
New claims stop being accepted and the card disappears from the app. Pairs already claimed are untouched: a friend keeps the $25 already on their account, and if their first order is paid while the switch is off, the referrer is not credited — the pair just waits as claimed until you turn it back on.
While off
No code is created even if someone opens their Account screen, so you won't accumulate half-live codes during the quiet week.
What it never does
Touch credit already granted, delete pairs, or message anyone.

Three rules I'd build it with

Once per order, then quiet
Show it on the delivered screen, and never re-show it for that order. "Not now" dismisses it for a week, not forever.
Skip anyone with a balance owing
Asking a favour of someone whose payment just failed reads badly. Collect first.
Same card, no second design
It's the Account card with a warmer headline and a dismiss link. One component, two mounts — so a copy change in Settings lands in both places at once.
